Research thesis

Distributed software can be studied as a dynamic, coupled system. Runtime telemetry provides observations; topology provides structure; spectral analysis can provide a compact description of system modes and potential fragility.

Goal: move reliability engineering from incident response toward measurable, predictive and adaptive resilience.

Experimental agenda

Validate proposed resilience signals against controlled workload ramps, fault injection, topology changes and recovery interventions.

Compare SRI-style predictors with conventional SLOs, observability signals and chaos-engineering outcomes.

Research Vision 2030

From SRE to Resilience Science

A long-term research direction: develop a reproducible mathematical language for software resilience that connects telemetry, graph structure, spectral modes, stress/strain analogies, phase transitions and autonomous remediation.
